Wake up at your stop

GPS Alarm rings when you reach a place, not a time. Set a destination, doze off on the train, and get woken at your stop — through Silent mode, Focus, and with the app closed.

How it works

  • Search for a place or press and hold the map, then choose how close counts as arrived (100 m to 5 km).
  • Saving arms the alarm. Up to 20 alarms can be armed at once.
  • When you enter the zone, a full-screen system alarm rings. It rings once, then stays on your list switched off, ready for next time.

Permissions the app needs

  • Location "Always" — so the alarm can wake the app while it is closed. Choose it when asked, or set it later in Settings › GPS Alarm › Location.
  • Precise Location — arrival zones need it; keep it on.
  • Alarms — lets the alarm ring through Silent mode and Focus.

If any of these is missing, the app shows a banner with a shortcut to Settings.

The alarm did not ring?

  • Check the banner in the app: location "Always", Precise Location and Alarms must all be allowed.
  • Arming an alarm while you are already inside its zone does not ring — it waits for your next arrival.
  • Underground, in tunnels or in Low Power Mode the ring can arrive late; iOS decides when it sees your location.
